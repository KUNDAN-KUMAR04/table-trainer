What we collect

  • Email address. We store only a one-way hash of it, plus a masked form (for example ra•••@gmail.com) shown in the phone sign-in list. It is not used for marketing and we send no emails.
  • Phone numbers. We store only one-way hashes and a masked form (+91 ••••1234). We never send SMS or call you.
  • Name. The name you enter. In groups, a teacher or group admin may change the name shown and add a school ID or roll number.
  • Certificate name. If you make a certificate, the real name you type is kept only in your browser on your device. It is not uploaded, not shown publicly, and not linked to your game name unless you choose to add the game name or User ID to a certificate you download.
  • Learning data. Scores, XP, streaks, accuracy, daily activity and badges.
  • Practice time. How many minutes you practise each day, used for the daily-time reminder.
  • Fair-play signals. To keep leaderboards honest we record how answers are given: response times, whether keyboard or touch input was used, whether the tab was hidden, and a trust score and tag (for example Novice, Pro, Max or Suspicious). We do not record the screen, keystroke contents, or anything outside Table Trainer. The developer can review these signals and may reduce XP or hide a player from leaderboards if play looks automated.
  • Public nickname. Each member of a guild can choose a nickname. It is shown in the guild's "Who paid" list and "Vault activity" instead of an email or phone, and on the guild's public page if the owner has turned the public page on. If you set no nickname we show "Member 1", "Member 2" and so on.
  • Vault activity. Coin payments to a guild vault are kept as one total per member per day (with a count of payments), for the last 14 days only. Older rows are deleted automatically.
  • Roles. Each member in a guild is a Super admin, an admin or a member. Super admins can change roles and perk prices. Admins can spend the vault and manage members.
  • Shop purchases. Items you buy with coins (avatars, titles, themes, power-ups) and, for guilds, items bought from the guild vault. Guild admins can see their own guild's recent purchases (who bought what and the cost); other members see the buyer's public nickname. No real money is involved.
  • Arena matches. Your Arena rating, win/loss record, recent match history and scores. While you search for a match, your display name, rating and title are visible to other players who are searching. Opponents see your guild's display name, not your email or phone.
  • Admin actions. When a Super admin changes a member's role, the change is recorded in a short audit log (last 40 entries) visible to that guild's admins.
  • Group data. Groups you join, your role, and your scores inside each group. Super admins and admins can see names, school IDs and scores of members of their group, and the masked email or phone of each member. Members can see names and scores of other members.
  • Anonymous device identifier. Firebase creates an anonymous ID for your browser so that your devices can be linked to your profile.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) and, for convenience, in your browser's local storage. Google processes it on our behalf.

Children

People under 13 (or the age required by local law) should only use Table Trainer with permission from a parent, guardian or school. Schools that create groups are responsible for obtaining any consent they need from parents. We do not show ads and do not sell data.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards (daily, monthly, yearly and all-time) show first name and last initial for individuals and the guild name for guilds. You can hide yourself in Settings. Group leaderboards are visible to group members.

Your choices

  • Edit your name and phone numbers in your account.
  • Leave any group at any time.
  • Delete your account from the account screen. This removes your profile, linked phone entries, leaderboard entry and group memberships. Group admins must first transfer or delete groups they own.
  • To ask about your data, email [your email address].

Security

There are no passwords or SMS codes. Access depends on knowing the email and a linked phone number, so do not share them. Scores are calculated in the browser, are checked by automatic fair-play rules, and should not be used for grades or prizes.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
